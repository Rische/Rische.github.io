Orbi Universo Team SARL

I worked on the projects listed below as a partner and employee of Orbi Universo Team SARL, the company founded by myself and three other colleagues.
Initially planned to market the games Orbi Universo and Ruins & Switch during my studies at Supinfogame Rubika, I then participated in the development of numerous titles within the company until 2025.

I was the main or sole programmer on all of these projects. I also sometimes take on the role of 3D artist and do some game design or user interface design.

I am leaving Orbi Universo Team SARL in 2025 to pursue new opportunities.
